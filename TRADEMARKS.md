# LeagueSphere Trademark Policy

The LeagueSphere software is free software under
[AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). This policy covers something the licence does
**not** grant: the LeagueSphere **name**, the **logo**, and the
**leaguesphere.app** domains.

Trademark law exists so that people can tell where a service comes from. It is
not here to stop you from using or improving the code — that is what the
licence is for.

## You may, without asking

- Run LeagueSphere for your league, club, association or company — public or
  internal — under any branding you like, as long as the public-facing product
  does not use our marks (see below).
- Fork the code, modify it, and publish your fork.
- Sell hosting, support or customisation built on the code.
- Say, accurately and without implying endorsement: *"a fork of
  LeagueSphere"*, *"based on LeagueSphere"*, or *"compatible with
  LeagueSphere"*.
- Keep internal names (module names, import paths, database names, commit
  messages) that reference LeagueSphere. Nobody audits your `import
  league_manager`.

## You must, if you distribute or host a fork publicly

**Rebrand.** A public deployment of modified code must not present itself as
LeagueSphere:

- Remove the LeagueSphere name and logo from the title, headers, login page,
  about page, emails, PDFs, error pages and social previews.
- Do not use `leaguesphere.app` or `demo.leaguesphere.app`, or a confusingly
  similar domain, as the primary address.
- Do not publish container images, npm packages or Python distributions under
  the `leaguesphere` namespace, or names that are confusingly similar.
- Do not imply that your deployment is official, supported, endorsed or
  operated by the LeagueSphere maintainers.

**Comply with the licence.** Publish the source of the exact version your users
interact with (AGPL §13). See
[docs/topics/guides/licensing.md](docs/topics/guides/licensing.md).

## Reserved marks

The following are reserved by the LeagueSphere maintainer:

- The word mark **LeagueSphere**.
- The LeagueSphere logo and any confusingly similar variant.
- The domains **leaguesphere.app** and **demo.leaguesphere.app**.
- The GitHub organisation/repository names and the `leaguesphere` Docker Hub
  namespace.
- The phrase **"official LeagueSphere"**, and any badge or claim of
  affiliation with the project.

Nothing here grants a trademark licence, by implication or otherwise.

## Unmodified redistribution

If you redistribute LeagueSphere **unmodified**, you may keep the name and
logo to describe what it is — for example a self-hosted instance whose users
should recognise the product — provided you do not suggest that the
maintainers run or endorse it. Keeping the "LeagueSphere" name for an
unmodified deployment does not require permission; modifying it does.

## Reporting misuse

If you believe a deployment is using the marks in a misleading way — for
example claiming to be the official service, or presenting a fork as the
original — contact the maintainer via the repository issue tracker. We prefer
a conversation to a letter.

## Questions

Open an issue at
<https://github.com/dachrisch/leaguesphere/issues> with the title
`Trademark:` and describe your case. Permission for a specific use is granted
in writing, case by case.
