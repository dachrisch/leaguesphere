# What's New 2026

Everything big that LeagueSphere learned to do this year — in plain language.

## What LeagueSphere already did coming into 2026

- Plan game days and leagues, with tables and standings calculated automatically.
- Design schedules visually with the Gameday Designer, using reusable templates.
- Enter scores on the field (Scorecard), follow games live (Liveticker) and
  write match reports.
- Manage clubs, rosters, referees and player eligibility (Passcheck).
- Dashboards to follow operations and usage.

## At a glance: before → now

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Scores lived only inside LeagueSphere | Publish fixtures, results, live scores and tables on your own club site |
| Schedules built by hand | Guided tours, one-click referee assignment, move games across stages, sensible defaults |
| Cup and tournament planning was basic | Dedicated tournament views, ready-made finals templates, legacy schedules migrated automatically |
| Referee paperwork was manual | Import self-reported games, automatic compliance checks, referee leaderboards |
| Rosters and stats stood alone | Linked with Passcheck: exports and names where you need them |
| Static overview pages | Live "upcoming / in progress" status on today's game days |

## Share your games on your own website

The biggest new capability this year: your games, on your site.

- **Embeddable widget** — show fixtures, results, live scores and standings in
  any club website with a simple embed code. Pick your teams, season and
  league; colors and logo adapt to your club.
- **Table view for several teams** — one embed shows every league table your
  teams play in, with your teams highlighted.
- **Better link previews** — shared LeagueSphere links now show proper titles,
  images and search-friendly data.

See [Share Widget](./share-widget/overview.md).

## Plan game days faster

- **Tournament support** — dedicated views for cups and tournaments, automatic
  time calculation, ranking stages and ready-made Finals 2026 templates.
- **Guided start** — onboarding tours walk you through building a schedule and
  saving it as a template.
- **Less clicking** — assign all referees with one button, move games between
  stages and fields instead of rebuilding, and set your own defaults for game
  length and breaks.
- **Expert inspector** — check exactly how teams progress through complex
  tournament formats; see [dual-progression](./dual-progression.md).
- **Your identity** — the designer shows your avatar and name, so teams always
  know who planned what.
- **Safer results** — game days with entered results can't be unlocked by
  accident, and lost schedules can be recovered.

## Referees and match reports, minus the paperwork

- **Import reported games** — referees' self-reported games (including
  spreadsheets) can be reviewed and imported through a guided staff workflow.
- **Automatic compliance check** — match reports verify officials and show
  license numbers by themselves.
- **Referee leaderboard and team cards** — searchable cards and statistics show
  who officiated what.
- **Passcheck where you need it** — export eligibility data from match reports
  and game-day views, and see Passcheck names in statistics.

## Follow your league day by day

- **Live status** — today's game days show whether they're upcoming or live,
  with clearer progress overviews.
- **Maintenance notices** — planned downtimes are announced in advance and
  shown clearly instead of failing silently.

## Good to know: safer and more reliable

- Logins and data transport are hardened; roster details stay private unless
  you're logged in.
- The system recovers by itself from short database hickups and deploys
  without downtime.
